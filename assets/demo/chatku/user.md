# Profile

## Liberal Studies & Career Exploration
The user is a first-year student in the Division of Liberal Studies at Kyung Hee University and has not yet chosen a major. They are weighing employment prospects against the experience of making something that other people find useful, and they are exploring user experience design as one possible direction without treating it as a settled career path.

## Interests & Working Style
They enjoy organizing information so that it is easy to follow, such as reordering the flow of a class presentation, adding examples, or restructuring a long club announcement so that dates and materials appear first. They find structuring content more enjoyable than choosing colors or polishing visuals. They are interested in observing where users get stuck, for example when looking for a registration button at a school event.

## Learning & Communication Preferences
Communicating in Korean, they prefer concise answers with concrete examples drawn from familiar settings such as campus events. They are cautious about claiming skills they have not yet tested and prefer to try small tasks before judging their aptitude.

## Current Activities
They plan to join a senior student's team that builds exhibition guide pages, starting with the guide page rather than photography, and to record which parts of the experience they enjoy and find difficult before deciding on a major.
